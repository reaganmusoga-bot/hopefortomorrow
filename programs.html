Program 01

Education — the key out of poverty.

Education is the single most powerful tool to break the cycle of generational poverty. We enroll every child in our care into formal schooling — covering fees, uniforms, books, meals and transport. But we don't stop at enrollment.

We provide after-school tutoring, exam preparation, learning materials at home, and a reading club every Saturday. Our team tracks each child's academic progress term by term and intervenes early if a child is falling behind.

Program 02

Nutrition & Health — because no child can learn hungry or sick.

Many of the children we rescue arrive malnourished, with untreated infections, and without basic immunizations. Our first job is to stabilize their health — then keep them thriving.

Every child in our care receives three balanced meals a day, clean drinking water, routine medical checkups, immunizations, deworming, and access to treatment for malaria, infections and chronic conditions. We also run a monthly community health clinic for families in Wakiso District.

Program 03

Safe Shelter — a real home, not an institution.

For orphans, abandoned children and former street kids, our shelter is not just a building — it is family. A place where they are known by name, tucked into bed at night, listened to, and loved unconditionally.

Our home in Wakiso District hosts children in a family-style environment with house parents who live on-site. Every child has their own bed, clean clothes, a study space, and access to counseling for the trauma they've endured.

A bed and a room — clean linens, mosquito nets, and a place to call their own.

House parents & caregivers — consistent, loving adults who act as family.

Counseling & healing — trauma-informed support to help children recover and thrive.

Program 04

Mentorship & Skills — preparing them to stand on their own.

Education is a launchpad — but real independence also requires life skills, confidence, and vocational training. As our children grow into teenagers and young adults, we walk with them into the next chapter.

Our Mentorship & Skills Program pairs each older child with a mentor, runs workshops on entrepreneurship, financial literacy and life skills, and provides vocational training in tailoring, carpentry, hairdressing, computer skills and agriculture — so they can earn a living and give back.

Our Approach

How We Work

A child's journey from rescue to independence — step by step.

Step 01

Identify

Our team and community partners find children in crisis — orphans, street kids, abandoned and abused children.

Step 02

Rescue

We bring them into a safe home, provide immediate medical care, food, clothing and a warm bed.

Step 03

Restore

Enroll in school, provide counseling, nutrition, healthcare and consistent love to heal and grow.

Step 04

Empower

Mentorship, vocational training and life skills — so they leave us ready to stand on their own.
