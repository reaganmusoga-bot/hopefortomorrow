Featured Story

From the Street to the Classroom

Education April 2025 · 4 min read

"I used to sell plastic bags on the street. Now I want to become a doctor."

When we met Sarah*, she was 9 years old, out of school, and helping her grandmother hawk plastic bags to survive. Her mother had passed away, and her grandmother — already elderly — couldn't afford school fees or food.

Within a week of our team meeting her, Sarah was enrolled in school, given a uniform, books, and a place in our home. Today, three years later, she is in Primary 5 — top of her class in mathematics — and dreams of becoming a doctor so she can help other children like her.

Her grandmother visits every month. "I never thought I would see her in a school uniform," she says. "Now she reads to me at night. God bless everyone who made this possible."

"Now she reads to me at night. God bless everyone who made this possible."

— Sarah's grandmother
