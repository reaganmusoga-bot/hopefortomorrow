We are Hope for Tomorrow Foundation.

A Ugandan-registered NGO on a mission to give the country's most vulnerable children what every child deserves — a safe home, a full stomach, an education, and a reason to hope.

Who We Are

A family built on love, not charity.

Hope for Tomorrow Foundation was born out of a simple, painful observation: children in our own community — Wakiso District — were being left behind. Orphaned by disease, abandoned on the streets, or trapped in a cycle of poverty they never chose.

We started with one child. Then five. Then fifty. Today, we walk with hundreds of children every day — providing not just the basics, but dignity, mentorship, and a real path out of poverty. We are not a charity handing out handouts. We are a family building futures.

What Drives Us

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To rescue, restore and empower vulnerable children in Uganda — providing education, nutrition, healthcare and shelter so they can grow into self-reliant, contributing members of society.

Our Vision

A Uganda where every child — no matter their birth, background or circumstances — has the opportunity to live a full, dignified and hope-filled life.

Our Values

  • Compassion — love first, always.
  • Integrity — every shilling accounted for.
  • Dignity — we serve, we don't pity.
  • Empowerment — hand up, not hand out.
  • Community — we rise together.
Our Journey

From One Child to Hundreds

Every milestone is a story of lives changed.

2018

A Calling

Our founders took in the first child — an orphaned 7-year-old girl — and started feeding neighborhood children from a single kitchen.

2019

A Home is Born

We opened our first shelter in Wakiso District, giving 12 children a safe place to sleep, eat and be loved.

2021

Education First

Partnered with local schools to enroll every child in formal education. Over 100 children started school that year.

2023

Recognized & Registered

Officially registered as an NGO with the Ugandan government. Began publishing annual transparency reports.

2024

Growing Community

Reached 500+ children supported across multiple programs. Launched international donor partnerships.

Today

The Mission Continues

We are building a permanent campus to house 200 children, train youth in vocational skills, and become a model of holistic child care in East Africa.

The Context

Why Uganda? Why now?

Uganda is home to over 2.5 million orphans — many losing parents to HIV/AIDS, malaria and poverty. Millions more live in extreme poverty, often on less than $1 a day. Child labor, child marriage and malnutrition remain daily realities.

Yet Uganda is also a country of immense potential — with a young population eager to learn, and communities that pull together. The children we serve are not statistics. They are future teachers, doctors, engineers and leaders. They just need someone to give them a chance.

"The best way to predict the future is to create it — one child at a time."

— Our founding principle
