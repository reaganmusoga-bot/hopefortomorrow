Our Mission

Giving Uganda's poorest children a real chance at life.

Founded in Wakiso District, Hope for Tomorrow Foundation exists to lift vulnerable children out of extreme poverty. Many of the children we serve are orphans, former street children, or from families earning less than $1 a day.

Through education, nutrition, medical care and mentorship, we walk with each child until they can stand on their own — and one day, give back to their community.

  • Education first

    School fees, uniforms, books and mentorship for every child.

  • Nutrition & health

    Daily meals, clean water and access to medical care.

  • Safe shelter

    A loving home for children with nowhere else to go.

What We Do

Our Core Programs

Four pillars that transform a child's life — from the classroom to the dinner table, from the clinic to a loving home.

Education

We enroll children in school, cover fees and supplies, and provide tutoring and mentorship so they can dream beyond their circumstances.

Nutrition & Health

Daily meals, clean water, immunizations and medical care — because no child can learn on an empty stomach or with untreated illness.

Safe Shelter

A loving home for orphans and street children — with house parents, counseling and a family atmosphere they can thrive in.

A Story of Hope

"I used to sell plastic bags on the street. Now I want to become a doctor."

When we met Sarah*, she was 9 years old, out of school, and helping her grandmother hawk plastic bags to survive. Today, she is in Primary 5, eats three meals a day, and dreams of becoming a doctor so she can help other children like her.

Her story is not unique — it is one of hundreds. Every child we take in is a life rewritten, a family lifted, a community changed.
