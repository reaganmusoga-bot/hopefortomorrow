Compliance & Policies

Policies We Live By

Child Protection & Safeguarding

Zero-tolerance for abuse. All staff and volunteers are vetted, trained and bound by our safeguarding policy.

Anti-Fraud & Whistleblowing

Anonymous reporting line for any suspicion of misuse. All reports are investigated by the board.

Donor Privacy & Data

We never sell, share or rent donor data. Payments are processed by PCI-compliant providers.

Conflict of Interest

All board and staff declare interests annually. Related-party transactions require board approval.

Environmental Responsibility

We minimize waste, source locally, and teach sustainable practices to the children in our care.

Community Participation

Local leaders, families and children help shape our programs. Nothing is designed in isolation.

Our Promise to You

The Donor's Bill of Rights

1

You have the right to know how your gift is used, and to receive regular updates on the children it supports.

2

You have the right to ask any question about our finances, governance or programs — and get a real answer.

3

You have the right to privacy. We never sell, trade or share your personal information.

4

You have the right to expect professional, ethical conduct from every person representing our foundation.

5

You have the right to cancel a recurring gift at any time, no questions asked.

6

You have the right to be treated with respect, whether you give $5 or $50,000.
